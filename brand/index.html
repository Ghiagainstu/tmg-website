TMG Brand Assets

Logos, colour and typography for partners, agencies and press representing Tuyue Media Gateway. Everything on this page is approved for use. If you need a format that is not listed here, ask and we will supply it.

Logo

The mark is a gold crescent formed by two overlapping circles: a full circle for reach, cut by a second for the gateway between markets. The cut is a transparent mask, not a coloured overlay — so the mark sits cleanly on any background colour, photograph or video. The icon sits to the left of the brand name, never above it in the horizontal lockup.

Clear space

Keep a margin equal to the height of the crescent, roughly one third of the mark width, clear on all four sides. No text, image edge or other logo enters that zone.

Minimum size

Digital: 24 px for the mark, 80 px for the full lockup. Print: 8 mm and 26 mm. Below this the crescent closes up and the wordmark loses legibility.

Wordmark specification

Wordmark is typeset, not drawn. TMG sits inside the 48×48 icon: Inter 800, 19 px, letter-spacing −0.8, anchored at x=4 y=32, in #f0f4ff on dark or #080c14 on light. The brand name sits to the right of the icon as live text: Inter 700, −0.02em, with Gateway in gold. Gap between icon and text equals the site spacing token, and the icon renders at 40 px in the header.

Do
  • Use the gold gradient on light or dark neutral backgrounds
  • Use a monochrome version when the background is busy or the medium is one-colour
  • Keep the crescent to the left of the wordmark
  • Use the icon alone only where the brand is already established in context
Don't
  • Do not stretch, squash, rotate or outline the mark
  • Do not recolour the crescent outside the palette below
  • Do not add drop shadows, glows or bevels
  • Do not place the gold gradient on a mid-tone or photographic background
  • Do not re-typeset the wordmark in another font or weight

Contrast rules

CombinationRatioVerdict
#C9A84C on #080C148.7 : 1Approved for all text sizes

#A8862F on #FFFFFF3.6 : 1Headings and large text only
#C9A84C on #FFFFFF2.2 : 1Do not use. Gold on white is illegible at body size. Step down to #A8862F.
#080C14 on #FFFFFF19.1 : 1Approved for all text sizes
The most common mistake in partner material is putting #C9A84C on a white slide. On a projector it reads as a pale yellow smudge. On white, always step down to #A8862F, and only for headings.

Colour pairing

Two approved themes. Digital decks and web use the dark theme. Printed or projected documents use the light theme. Do not mix them inside one deliverable.

Dark theme · web, deck, video
Deep navy canvas, gold accent, no third colour
Body copy in Text Primary. Supporting text and sources drop to Muted. Gold marks the one thing you want remembered.
#080C14 · #C9A84C · #F0F4FF · #8899B5
Light theme · print, PowerPoint, PDF
White canvas, ink text, gold deep for emphasis
Headings in Deep Navy. Body in near-black grey. Gold is used sparingly, for the single number or word that carries the point.
#FFFFFF · #080C14 · #A8862F · #333A45

60 / 30 / 10

Roughly 60% background, 30% text and structure, 10% gold. If gold exceeds a tenth of the visible area it stops being an accent and starts looking decorative.

One gold element per view

A slide or screen should carry one thing in gold. Two competing gold elements means neither reads as the point.
