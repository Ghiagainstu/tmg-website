Token Went on Sale at Double 11: The +450% Category That Has Not Started Selling Yet

For the first time, a Double 11 product list includes a category with no colour, no size, no warehouse, no shipping and no tracking number. It is called Token — formally 词元, the term the National Data Administration standardised in 2026 — and it is the unit a model bills for every piece of text it processes. Seven model vendors were on Tmall by 15 October, aggregated under an AI Space Station entry, with JD running a hardware-attach model and Douyin largely absent. 1. What Went on Sale

For the first time, a Double 11 product list includes a category with no colour, no size, no warehouse, no shipping and no tracking number. It is called Token — formally 词元, the term the National Data Administration standardised in 2026 — and it is the unit a model bills for every piece of text it processes.

The rollout was staged over two months:

DateEvent
2 September 2026Zhipu opens a Tmall flagship store, selling Coding Plan subscriptions
3 September 2026Tmall launches the AI Token recharge centre; first batch: Alibaba Cloud, Zhipu, Kimi, MiniMax, DeepSeek
September 2026Alibaba Cloud and Qianwen Office open official stores
13 October 2026MiniMax opens the first brand flagship; three tiers at RMB 49 / 119 / 469 list, all half price during Double 11, lowest tier RMB 24.50
15 October 2026Tmall's press conference confirms seven model vendors: Alibaba Cloud Bailian, MiniMax, Qianwen Office, Moonshot, Qoder, Zhipu, StepFun
20 October 2026Double 11 opens; the category is live

Tmall also added an aggregate entry point, AI空间站 ("AI Space Station"), listing monthly, quarterly and annual cards across vendors on one page. Tmall president Jia Luo's stated goal is to assemble the full matrix of Chinese model companies' Token sales entry points.

2. The +450% Number Needs a Caveat

The headline figure is real: the Token category, appearing at Double 11 for the first time, grew 450% versus the previous month. AI PC, a related category, grew over 300% in its first hour.

Both numbers are platform disclosures on a base that did not exist a month earlier. The same coverage that reports the growth rate reports the volume:

StoreReported volume
Zhipu Tmall flagship, all Token products combinedroughly 300 units
MiniMax Tmall flagship, since launch100+ units
Zhipu GLM Coding Plan Lite monthly card on JD"100+ sold"

A category growing 450% from a standing start, with store-level counts in the low hundreds, is a category that has not started selling yet. It is a category that has started being counted. Both facts matter, and the second is the more useful one for planning.

3. How It Is Priced, Platform by Platform

The three platforms have made three different bets on the same product.

PlatformMechanismExamples
TmallToken as an independent category with its own display spaceMiniMax M Plan from RMB 24.50 (first order, half price); Qianwen Office Lite RMB 39 / Standard RMB 139 / Pro RMB 499; Zhipu Coding Plan at list price
JDToken as a hardware attach plus an aggregated service stationOther brands' Tokens from RMB 0.99; JD Cloud JoyAgent points pack from RMB 9; the "京补合约" bundle — buy a Huawei watch or learning machine on contract, main item up to RMB 120 off, add a 12-month Token pack (12 months of DeepSeek 20M tokens, Kimi 20M or GLM 6M) from RMB 30 per month
DouyinWeakest presence; no independent Token venueOnly scattered traces of a RMB 9.90 monthly pack, with users reporting the allowance runs out before one task finishes

Tmall's packaging follows consumer-goods logic rather than API logic. Qianwen Office bundles its tiers by scenario — study, home, research, one-person studio — in the way cosmetics sets are assembled. MiniMax and Alibaba Cloud sell monthly, quarterly and annual cards plus scenario bundles, converting linear per-million-token billing into prepaid packages.

4. The Discount Reality: Three Yuan

The gap between the campaign framing and the shelf is where the story actually sits.

Reporters who contacted JD Cloud, Tmall MiniMax and Alibaba Cloud storefronts found that most stores had no Token products participating in Double 11 promotions at all. Zhipu was one of the few genuinely discounting: its GLM Coding Plan Lite monthly card moved from RMB 118 to RMB 115 on JD — a reduction of three yuan — with a Tmall opening-day flash price from RMB 116.90. Alibaba Cloud and MiniMax customer service confirmed their Token products were excluded from the campaign.

At the platform level there is no subsidy either. Tmall president Jia Luo was direct about it: "On Token we have no investment." Tmall's role is traffic and transaction infrastructure; the discount cost sits with the vendors and carriers.

So the most discounted AI product on China's largest shopping festival is three yuan cheaper than its own website. That is not a pricing story. It is a distribution story.

5. Who the Target Buyer Is

Jia Luo's stated target profile is not the general consumer: individuals who already call models regularly — for market research, family study, a one-person studio or a small team — and who today pay separately at each vendor's own website.

The purchase experience is built to match. Beijing Daily's reporter tested it: searching a model brand on Tmall produces a page that behaves like mobile top-up — select a package, enter a phone number, pay, and the allowance lands on the account immediately. Nothing ships.

6. Why Vendors Are Doing It: Cash Flow, Not Margin

The strategic rationale is the part most coverage skips, and it is the part that explains why a category with thin volume and no discount exists at a festival built on discounts.

Model vendors' margins are falling while usage explodes. Domestic daily Token consumption grew from roughly 100 trillion tokens per day at the end of 2025 to over 500 trillion by June 2026. Over the same period:

VendorGross margin
Zhipu, full year 202541.0%
Zhipu, first half 202626.4%
MiniMax, full year 202525.4%
Anthropic, inference margin (Barclays estimate, for comparison)~65%

CICC estimates the theoretical margin of a self-built, list-price-selling "token factory" at about 60%. The gap between theory and reality comes from rigid cost on one side and a price war on the other: lightweight model input pricing has fallen to around RMB 0.30 per million tokens, while flagship output ranges from single digits to above RMB 30.

Against that background, an e-commerce prepaid package does something a metered API bill cannot: it converts volatile usage revenue into prepaid, dated cash flow. The Double 11 listing is less a sales channel than a user-education experiment — teaching individuals who pay per call to think of AI consumption the way they think of a phone plan.

7. The Margin Squeeze Behind the Category

Two adjacent figures give the category its economic context, and both point the same way.

Carrier token packages arrived first: China Telecom has sold a light tier at RMB 9.90 for 10 million tokens since May. Standardised compute packs have cut the cost of generating one minute of AI video from fifty to sixty RMB to about one RMB.

That is the direction of travel for input costs, and it is why the e-commerce listings are not primarily margin plays. The value to a vendor is a locked-in customer and cash up front; the value to the platform is a new category that signals AI is for consumers. Neither side needs the unit economics to work this quarter.

8. Platform Differentiation: Three Different Bets

PlatformBetReading
TmallToken as a retail category with brand flagships and an aggregate venueBuilding the shelf; volume is a later problem
JDToken as a hardware attach on 3C contractsUsing existing 3C subsidy logic to acquire the first wave of paying users
DouyinMinimal involvementNo independent venue; the consumption scenario does not fit its content-led model

The divergence is informative. The same product has been merchandised three ways in the same week, and the differences track each platform's existing strengths rather than any property of the product.

9. What This Means for Brands

DevelopmentConsequence for a brand
AI subscriptions are now a shoppable retail categoryIf you sell an AI product in China, category placement and promotion design are open questions, not settled ones
Most vendors ran no festival discountThe market has not settled on whether AI subscriptions are promotion-eligible goods; a discount strategy is available precisely because almost nobody is using one
Tmall did not subsidise the categoryPlatform support must be earned through category positioning, not assumed
JD bundles Token into hardware contractsDistribution partnerships can reach buyers that a standalone listing cannot
Per-token prices keep falling while vendor margins compressSubscription packaging is a hedge against falling unit prices, and a reason to lock customers in early
Tool access is becoming commoditised at RMB 24.50Competitive differentiation shifts from "which AI you can afford" to "whether AI answers with your brand in it"

That last row is the one with the longest tail. When every competitor can buy the same model for the price of a takeaway meal, the differentiator is not the tool but whether the model has your information to work from.

11. Key Takeaways

  • Token appeared as a Double 11 category for the first time in 2026, sold as monthly, quarterly and annual subscriptions with no physical fulfilment.
  • Seven model vendors were on Tmall by the 15 October press conference: Alibaba Cloud Bailian, MiniMax, Qianwen Office, Moonshot, Qoder, Zhipu and StepFun, aggregated under an "AI Space Station" entry.
  • The +450% growth figure is platform-disclosed and on a near-zero base; store-level counts are in the low hundreds of units.
  • Most vendors ran no festival discount. The deepest observed cut was RMB 3 on a RMB 118 plan, and Tmall's president stated the platform has "no investment" in the category.
  • Tmall, JD and Douyin made three different bets: retail category, hardware attach, and minimal involvement respectively.
  • JD's "京补合约" bundles 12-month Token packs (DeepSeek 20M, Kimi 20M or GLM 6M tokens at RMB 30 per month) into 3C hardware contracts — using existing subsidy logic to acquire first paying users.
  • Vendor margins are compressing while usage explodes: domestic daily token consumption rose from ~100 trillion to over 500 trillion between late 2025 and June 2026, while Zhipu's gross margin fell from 41.0% (2025) to 26.4% (H1 2026) and MiniMax ran at 25.4% (2025).
  • Prepaid packages convert volatile metered revenue into dated cash flow — the commercial rationale behind listings that are not yet volume businesses.
  • Carrier packs set the cost floor: China Telecom has sold RMB 9.90 for 10 million tokens since May, and standardised packs cut one minute of AI video from ~RMB 55 to ~RMB 1.
  • Jia Luo's target buyer is not the general consumer but individuals already calling models regularly — market research, family study, one-person studios and small teams.
  • This article summarises platform announcements and media reporting for information purposes and is not investment or commercial advice. Vendor pricing, category structures and promotion mechanics change quickly; verify current terms before making procurement or budget decisions.
