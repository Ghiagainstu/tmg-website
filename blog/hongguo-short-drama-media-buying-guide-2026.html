Hongguo Short Drama: The Media Buyer's Guide to 168 Million Daily Users

Hongguo is a Douyin Group free short-drama app whose 168 million daily actives exceed the combined daily actives of Tencent Video, iQiyi, Youku and Mango TV. It ranks 18th by monthly active users and 4th by total time spent, and those two numbers measure different things. Inventory is sold through Juliang Engine at a RMB 25 CPM rate card, though traded prices run from about 14 to 20 yuan. The decision that matters most is not whether to buy but which content type to buy: real-actor drama and AI anime drama reach different people and convert to different outcomes.

1. What Hongguo Is, and What It Is Not

Hongguo (红果短剧) is a free short-drama app owned by Douyin Group, built inside ByteDance's Fanqie Novel team. It is not a start-up that got lucky; it is a product that inherited an audience, an IP library, a distribution channel and an ad salesforce before it launched.

StageDateWhat happened
Brand createdJuly 2021北京笔墨留香科技有限公司, a Douyin Group entity, registers the Hongguo brand
Internal testMay 2023Runs inside Douyin as "Hongguo Free Short Drama"
Standalone appAugust 2023Launches as a separate app
Model—Briefly tried paid membership, then went permanently free with ads

The innovation was in settlement, not in content. Paid short drama required producers to buy traffic on Douyin, with ByteDance taking a toll on the way past. Hongguo made the platform the destination instead: producers became suppliers settled on a revenue-share basis, and ByteDance captured both the toll and the destination.

The loop is closed inside one company:

  • Fanqie Novel supplies IP from its web-novel library
  • Jimeng and similar AI tools push production cost down
  • Hongguo holds the audience and the ad inventory
  • Douyin provides the traffic entry point and the transaction link

For a media buyer, the practical consequence is that Hongguo inventory is sold through Juliang Engine (巨量引擎), the same salesforce that sells Douyin. Budgets, targeting data and measurement sit in one ecosystem.

2. Two Rankings, and Why You Need Both

Hongguo is routinely described as "China's fourth-largest app." That is true of one metric and false of another, and a buyer needs to know which is which.

MetricHongguo's positionValue
Monthly active users18th368 million (QuestMobile, 2026 H1)
Total time spent4thBehind Douyin, WeChat and Douyin Lite

Both numbers come from QuestMobile. They are not in conflict; they measure different things. Rank by MAU measures reach. Rank by total time measures depth of engagement. Hongguo is mid-table on reach and near the top on depth, which is precisely why it is interesting as an advertising surface and also why its limits are what they are.

For context, the MAU leaderboard runs WeChat 1.154bn, Douyin 1.029bn, Taobao 983m, Alipay 952m, Amap 913m, Pinduoduo 703m, Sogou Input 698m, JD 677m, Baidu 640m, QQ 627m. Hongguo sits at 368m, one place behind Toutiao and one ahead of QQ Browser.

The daily-active comparison is the one that matters for planning. Hongguo's 168 million DAU exceeds the combined DAU of Tencent Video (~50m), iQiyi (~45m), Youku (~30m) and Mango TV (~30m), which totals roughly 155 million. One free short-drama app has more daily users than four long-video platforms added together.

3. How the Inventory Is Actually Sold

Hongguo runs an IAA model — in-app advertising. Users pay nothing; advertisers pay for exposure. Advertising accounted for 97% of revenue, with core revenue of roughly RMB 20 billion in H1 2026.

PlacementFormatBuyer note
Splash (开屏)Full screen on app openStrong for reach and awareness; best for introducing a brand to a new audience
Mid-roll (中插)Appears inside an episode, 5 seconds forced, cannot be closedThe highest-value position. Can deep-link to a Douyin livestream room or an e-commerce page
Floating overlay (悬浮贴片)Persistent corner unit, links to productWeakest conversion; needs an existing purchase context to work

Targeting runs on three audience pools: broad (泛人群), behavioural (行为人群) and search (搜索人群). Agency guidance consistently steers buyers to the latter two, because behavioural and search signals are computed through Yuntu (巨量云图), ByteDance's audience platform — for example, users who searched a category on Douyin or engaged with related content are scored as prospects and can then be reached on Hongguo.

That cross-app data flow is the single most useful commercial detail in this article: you are not buying an isolated app audience, you are buying a slice of Douyin's behavioural graph delivered on a cheaper surface.

4. The First Split: Real-Actor Drama vs AI Anime Drama

Treating Hongguo as one inventory pool is the most common buying mistake. The platform contains two content types with materially different audiences and materially different conversion profiles.

Real-actor drama (真人短剧)AI anime drama (AI 漫剧)
Core audienceWomen 31–40, 61–68% of the segmentMen 18–34, about 62%
Strongest outcomesHigh e-commerce conversion, high order volume, high lead captureGame downloads, app installs, novel subscriptions — TGI markedly higher
Weaker outcomes—Direct e-commerce purchase
Paid behavioursSubscription and one-off purchasesGame top-ups, novel subscriptions

DataEye's vice-president Lin Qiwen has stated that advertisers are already splitting budgets between the anime-drama zone and the real-actor zone rather than mixing them. That is the operational recommendation in one sentence: match inventory to KPI, and do not average the two.

If your client's conversion event is a purchase, buy real-actor drama. If it is an install, an activation or a subscription, buy anime drama. Mixing produces a blended number that flatters neither.

5. Where the Audience Is, and When

The audience has shifted faster than most published profiles admit. Hongguo's official early portrait skewed old; the 2026 reality is younger and more male.

Two things follow.

First, the "elderly audience" framing is out of date as a planning assumption. It was true at launch, when free access pulled in people the subscription paywall had excluded. It is no longer the whole picture: 45+ is now roughly 43%, and nearly half of new users arrive through the anime-drama funnel, which is young and male.

Second, spending power is thin. About 60% of middle-aged and older users have never paid for short drama, and 92% spend RMB 50 or less per month, including zero. That does not matter for an exposure buy and matters a great deal for any plan that assumes transaction value from this audience.

Timing is the practical surprise: the activity peak is 6am to 12pm, markedly higher than evening. Planning that assumes a long-video evening prime time will misallocate dayparting.

6. What Exposure Actually Delivers

The agency positioning is explicit: Hongguo "is suited to brand advertising." The contract delivers exposure, not orders.

What you are buyingWhat it means in practice
Guaranteed impression deliveryThe ad is shown; there is no commitment to a downstream action
Forced 5-second mid-roll attentionHigh certainty that the creative was on screen, in a lean-forward context
No purchase contextThe user is watching drama, not shopping; conversion depends on the landing experience, not the placement
Deep-link to Douyin commerceAvailable on mid-roll, which is what makes the position worth its premium

A commonly circulated agency model illustrates the arithmetic and its limits: RMB 2,500 buys 100,000 impressions; at a 2% click rate and 5% conversion with RMB 100 margin per order, that implies 50 orders and RMB 5,000 revenue — a theoretical ROI of 2. The same material cautions that real delivery varies by 15–30% because fill rates fluctuate and conversion differs sharply by drama title.

Treat that model as a sanity check, not a forecast. The honest reading is that Hongguo is a reach and frequency buy with unusually certain viewability, and any conversion assumption must be validated against your own landing page rather than the placement.

7. The Pricing Reality Behind the Rate Card

The quoted rate is not the traded rate, and the gap matters at scale.

ItemFigure
Rate card CPMRMB 25 per thousand impressions
Minimum commitment100,000 impressions/day ≈ RMB 2,500/day
Real-actor drama, tradedtypically below RMB 20 per thousand
AI anime drama, tradedas low as ~RMB 14 per thousand
Discount behaviourDeepens when inventory is oversold

Two consequences for planning. First, budget the traded rate, not the card rate — assuming RMB 25 overstates cost by 20–45%. Second, the discount is not a signal of quality; it reflects inventory saturation, which is itself a warning about how much supply exists relative to demand.

8. Three Constraints a Buyer Should Price In

A plan that ignores these will look fine in the media plan and fail in the review.

ConstraintWhat is happeningWhy it affects your buy
Creator economics are collapsingAnime-drama payout per ten thousand plays fell from RMB 20 to RMB 1–2; real-actor payouts fell from triple digits to single digitsSupply is being squeezed. Cheaper inventory now may mean thinner content variety later
Hit rates are extreme221,900 AI drama titles launched on Douyin in H1 2026; 0.48% passed 100m plays; only 1.3% reached break-evenContent quality is highly variable, so placement-level performance will vary far more than category averages suggest
Incentive mechanics are being withdrawnCoin rewards stop for new users from 1 November 2026, fully off by end-DecemberDaily actives include users who opened the app to claim rewards. Removing the incentive tests how much of the 168 million is genuine viewing intent

Regulation is the fourth, and it is already live. The Short Drama Development Measures (广电总局令第16号) took effect 1 September 2026 and require classification filing, labelling of AI-generated content, and a ban on algorithm models that induce addiction or excessive spending. Enforcement has been heavy: 68,000 titles removed network-wide this year, over 1,200 accounts actioned, more than 90% of removed content AI-generated, and Hongguo itself disposed of 20,426 low-quality or homogenised titles as of 27 October.

For an advertiser this is not just a compliance question. Titles get pulled, and a placement inside a pulled title disappears. Ask how inventory is reallocated when content is removed.

The cleanest way to think about Hongguo is as the cheap end of ByteDance's attention market. Same data, same salesforce, same measurement, materially lower CPM, materially lower purchase intent.

11. Key Takeaways

  • Hongguo is a Douyin Group product built by the Fanqie Novel team, launched as a standalone app in August 2023; inventory is sold through Juliang Engine, the same salesforce as Douyin.
  • It ranks 18th by monthly active users (368 million) and 4th by total time spent. Reach is mid-table; engagement depth is near the top.
  • Its 168 million daily actives exceed the combined daily actives of Tencent Video, iQiyi, Youku and Mango TV (roughly 155 million).
  • The model is IAA: advertising accounts for 97% of revenue, about RMB 20 billion in H1 2026, from users who pay nothing.
  • Rate card is RMB 25 CPM with a RMB 2,500/day minimum, but traded prices run below RMB 20 for real-actor drama and around RMB 14 for AI anime drama.
  • Mid-roll is the position worth buying — five seconds forced, cannot be closed, and it can deep-link to Douyin commerce. Splash suits awareness; overlays convert poorly.
  • The single most important operational split is real-actor vs AI anime drama. Real-actor skews women 31–40 and converts to purchases and leads; anime skews men 18–34 and converts to installs, activations and subscriptions.
  • Advertisers are already separating budgets across the two zones rather than mixing them, which is the right approach and the one to copy.
  • The audience is younger than its reputation. Users 45+ fell to roughly 43% in 2026, male share is near 60%, and 49.2% of new users arrive via anime drama.
  • Peak usage is 6am to 12pm, not evening — a dayparting fact that changes the plan.
  • Spending power is thin: about 60% of middle-aged and older users have never paid, and 92% spend RMB 50 or less monthly.
  • Four constraints to price in: collapsing creator payouts, a 1.3% break-even rate across AI titles, withdrawal of coin incentives from November 2026, and an active regulatory regime that removes titles in volume.
  • The honest positioning is reach, not conversion. Hongguo is the cheap end of ByteDance's attention market: same data, lower CPM, lower purchase intent.
  • This article is a market summary for media planning purposes and is not investment or procurement advice. Rate cards, traded prices and audience figures change monthly; confirm current numbers with Juliang Engine and your agency before committing budget.
